❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?
Start with 14 days of Pro features completely free. No credit card required to start. After the trial, you can choose to upgrade or continue with the free plan.
Can I change plans anytime?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any billing adjustments.
What is Luna Vision RAG™?
Luna Vision RAG™ is our premium feature that lets you analyze screenshots and compare them with your codebase. Simply upload a screenshot and ask questions about how it relates to your implementation.
Do you offer educational discounts?
Yes! We offer 50% off for students and educators. Contact us at education@lunaos.ai with your academic email for verification.
Is my code secure?
Absolutely. All code processing is encrypted and we never store your source code. We only maintain indexed representations for search functionality.
What integrations are available?
Luna RAG integrates seamlessly with Claude Desktop, Windsurf, Cline, and other MCP-compatible platforms. Pro users also get API access for custom integrations.
