Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try Pro for free?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial of Pro with no credit card required. Cancel anytime.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Absolutely! You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades are immediate, downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise plans.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fees for Free and Pro plans. Enterprise plans may have custom onboarding fees.

What happens when I hit the free tier limits?

You'll receive a notification and can upgrade to Pro to continue using Luna Agents without interruption.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. No questions asked.

Can I use Luna Agents offline?

Luna RAG works completely offline. Luna Vision RAG™ requires internet for cloud processing.

Is my code secure?

Yes! Luna RAG runs locally. Luna Vision RAG™ uses encrypted connections and doesn't store your code.
